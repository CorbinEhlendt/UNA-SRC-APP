# UNA-SRC-APP
> An app for students that are new or experienced in the SRC and what it has to offer.

### Authorship 

`@corbinehlendt` & `@carsonkerr` 

### User Story

As Students at UNA who work out, we want to create an app for helping beginners as well as experienced gym goers
navigate the SRC by giving them tools to better know when the best times to go are, what workouts they could get in within certain time limits,
and more.

### The Why

The gym can be a very intimidating place for people who have never been to a gym as well as for people who are used to other gyms. With this app,
we want to give students interested in going to the SRC the tools to feel as comfortable and ready as they can be. With our app we want to be able to 
track how busy the SRC is, be able to build workout plans for student who have a busy schedule and only have limited time to be there, build a plan for newcomers 
who are just getting started. This app could be a great tool for people just starting at the gym by giving them an idea of what to expect or what to do, as well as 
people who are experienced in the gym but want to know how busy the SRC is to help find a time that is best for them.
