# UNA-SRC-APP
> An app to help students manage their time with the SRC

### Authorship 

`@corbinehlendt` & `@carsonkerr` 

### User Story

 - As Students at UNA who work out,
 - we want to create an app to help students manage their time around working out at the SRC,
 - so that they feel more willing and prepared for working out.

### The Problem
The SRC At UNA doesn't currently have any app for students.<br>
For new students or students that have never been in the SRC gym may be overwhelmed and not know what to expect.<br>
We want to create an app for students interested in going to the SRC. The app would be able to <br>
tell users how busy the gym currently is as well as be able to use AI to help students get an idea of <br>
a workout they could implement around their school schedule based on what the SRC provides.

### The Value
For Students, our app helps them feel less overwhelmed with the gym because it allows them to have  <br>
more knowledge of how many people are their as well as creating a plan to help them feel more prepared.

### What Already exists
 - [Hevy App](https://www.hevyapp.com/) 
    - Hevy is basically just a logging app for people who already know what exercises they need to do. It does not generate a plan for users, it's just a way to track your progress.
 - [UAB Rec App](https://www.uab.edu/students/universityrecreation/about/mobile-app)
    - The UAB Rec App looks like it has many features, the one that looks the most relevant to us is a schedule. Their app shows different events or classes going on in their rec center which would be useful. We want to         replicate this and potentially have notifications that let users know if something is going on that they may be interested in.
 - [Planet Fitness Crowd Meter](https://pfmembership.com/planet-fitness-crowd-meter/)
    - The crowd meter uses real time check-ins as well as historical data to give users an idea how how busy certain times are at the gym by checking the app on their phone. This is a great tool that we would like
      to replicate on our app for Students here at UNA. 
 - [FitBod App](https://fitbod.me/)
    - FitBod differs from Hevy where it is an AI-driven app that generates a personalized plan for you. We could take inspiration from this for our SRC app.

### Done
 The main focuses for Kilo is to have a functioning "busy meter" to see how busy the SRC is as well as a functioning <br>
 AI helper that can create a workout for students who are busy and have a limited time frame to get in a workout.
